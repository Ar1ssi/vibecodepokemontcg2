# Poison — how it reads

**Identity:** glossy violet-magenta liquid with weight — globs that fly, splatter, pool, bubble and
drip — under a sickly yellow-green gas; acid is the same liquid in amber.

## Physical truth
Poison is a viscous liquid: opaque or nearly so, glossy (a strong specular dot), heavier than
water (slower arcs, thicker drips, globs that keep their shape), and it *stays*: it pools on the
target, bubbles, and drips off. Its gas is light-weight matter: soft, rising, warm-sick in hue
(yellow-green or lilac), thinning slowly. Poison is matter drawn over the scene; only its gas glow
and the bubble glints are light.

## Shape language
- Globs: rounded, slightly irregular blobs with a specular dot and a dark rim, varied sizes, in a
  volley with a slow arc; they stretch slightly along their velocity.
- Splatter and pool: a soft-edged irregular pool on the target with bubbles rising and popping
  in it; drips running off the lower edge; **no stroked outline** (a past clip-art verdict).
- Gas: soft round clouds, rising, with a faint inner glow; spores for powder moves (tiny dots).
- Acid: an orange-yellow spray that splashes and smokes (steam-like puffs) on the target.
- Chains (Malignant Chain): magenta-violet links with a white-cyan highlight forming a cage.

## Value & light (glossy matter + gas glow)
Glob: lit side body violet `#9F4492` / `#8B2BB8` → shadow side deep `#4A1060` → rim dark → one
white specular dot (the only white) and a magenta Fresnel rim `#D769DD`. Pool: body at ≤ 0.75
alpha over the card with a soft rim, bubbles with a highlight. Gas: `#9ACD32`-ish yellow-green or
lilac `#D08AAC`, ≤ 0.45 alpha, a faint additive inner glow. Acid: amber body `#FFB13B`, deep
`#C25A12`, hot `#FFE066`, with white-grey smoke.

## Palette (sampled)
- deep `#4A1060` · body `#9F4492` · hot `#D769DD` · rim `#FF00E6` · highlight `#B7F3FE` (Malignant Chain, Sludge Bomb)
- Gas: yellow-green `#B8E04A` → `#7FA82A`; lilac gas `#D08AAC`.
- Acid: `#C25A12 #FFB13B #FFE066` + smoke `#E8EEF5`.
- Drift to avoid: toward dragon/psychic clean violet light (poison is glossy *matter*), toward
  water's translucent blue, or a hard stroked pool rim.

## Motion signature
- Charge (200–400 ms): globs swell at the source (mouth, hands), bubbles rise around the attacker,
  a sick glow gathers.
- Travel: a volley of 8–12 globs in a slowish arc (250–400 ms), stretching along their paths,
  leaving small drips; a spray fans out as a cone of droplets.
- Contact: splatter — globs flatten into a pool on the target's face, droplets thrown off with
  gravity, a small magenta flash (tinted), gas puffs up.
- Dissipation (long): the pool bubbles (bubbles grow, pop into small splashes), drips run off the
  lower edge and fall, the gas rises and thins, the pool dissolves late with a soft rim.
- Poison never fades evenly: it drips away.

## What sells it
- Globs as lit spheres (water-drop kind in the poison palette with reduced clarity) with a
  strong specular and a dark rim; velocity stretch in flight; slow gravity arcs.
- Splatter as a soft-rimmed decal (matter, over) on the card's face, with a bubble layer (small
  spheres with highlights, growing then popping).
- Drips as thin tapering tubes running off the target's lower edge, falling as drops.
- Gas as smoke sprites in the sick palette with a faint additive glow layer inside.
- Spores as many tiny dots with slow drift for powder moves.

## Card motion & sound
`brace` / `rear-lurch` with a violet rim; the target `knock` lightly with a lingering violet veil
(poison lingers; the status loop takes over after). Sound: a wet splat transient, a bubbling tail,
a hiss for gas and acid.

## Not its neighbour
- vs **Ghost**: ghost's smoke is dry, cold violet, and weightless; poison's gas is warm-sick and
  its liquid has weight and gloss.
- vs **Water**: water is translucent saturated blue with soft foam; poison is opaque glossy violet
  with a specular dot and bubbles.
- vs **Dragon / Psychic**: their violet is light; poison's is matter.
- vs **Ground (mud)**: mud is brown and matte-glossy; sludge is violet-magenta and bubbles.

## Reference cues (SV)
- Acid: an orange-yellow spray that splashes and smokes on the target.
- Sludge / Sludge Bomb / Sludge Wave: magenta-violet sludge splatters on the target.
- Toxic / Poison Powder: violet spores and a sick haze settle on the target.
- Malignant Chain: a magenta-violet chain cage with white-cyan highlights, a pink-violet burst.

## Failure signatures seen here → fix
- Acid drawn purple → amber tint (the reference is orange-yellow).
- The sludge pool on the target with a hard stroked outline (clip art) → a soft rim, kit-wide.
- A poison "beam" as a clean violet light slab → a stream of globs, or a thick glossy liquid
  stream with a specular stripe.
