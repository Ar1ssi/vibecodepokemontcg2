# Normal — how it reads

**Identity:** colourless force: white-gold stamps, rings, speed lines and starbursts with no
element texture; the one place a *tinted* white is allowed as a body, and still never a slab.

## Physical truth
Normal attacks are plain kinetic hits (tackle, slam, scratch) and raw "energy" (Hyper Beam,
Judgment, Tera Starstorm). The reference draws kinetic hits as impact stars with speed lines and a
puff of dust, and raw energy as warm white-gold light: gold orbs, gold beams and pillars, gold
rings. Signature normals bring a hue (Techno Blast violet-white, Relic Song pastel, Tera Starstorm
teal-blue), but the default is white warmed toward gold with an orange edge.

## Shape language
- Impact stars: spiky four- or eight-point starbursts at the contact point with radial speed lines;
  a dust puff for body slams.
- Scratches and slashes: 2–3 parallel white claw streaks (Scratch, Fury Swipes) in quick succession.
- Giant hand / limb (Crush Grip): a huge translucent white hand closing on the target.
- Orbs and beams: a gold orb charged under a gold ring, gold beams or pillars raining from above
  (Judgment); a white-gold beam with a gold halo (Hyper Beam).
- Rings: expanding gold rings at impact (not spinning ring arcs round a charge — a past verdict).
- Stat/sound moves: soft pastel rings (Relic Song) with music notes.

## Value & light (additive, warm white)
Core `#FFFFFF` small → hot cream `#FFF5B0` / `#FFE066` → body gold `#F2C230` / `#FFB347` → deep
orange `#FF7A1A` / `#E8552B` at the edges. The body is *gold*, not white: a white body is the
slab the user keeps rejecting. Speed lines are thin and brief. Bloom on the core and a few sparks.

## Palette (sampled)
- deep `#E8552B` · body `#F2C230` · hot `#FFF5B0` · core `#FFFFFF` (Crush Grip, Multi-Attack)
- Judgment `#FFF3A6 #FFB347 #FF7A1A #FFE066`; Techno Blast violet `#A34BFF #E9B8FF` + cyan `#66E6FF`;
  Relic Song pastels `#FFB3E6 #B3E8FF #C7F5A8 #FFF3A8`; Tera Starstorm `#5FF2E0 #2FB8FF #E6FFFF #FFE07A`.
- Drift to avoid: pure white everything; or borrowing fighting's orange-brown shards (normal stays
  cleaner and lighter).

## Motion signature
- Charge (150–300 ms): a crouch and a warm rim; for energy moves a gold orb grows at the source
  with a gold ring *standing* around it (not spinning arcs).
- Drive: a `lunge` or `dash` for tackles; a beam fires in a frame; pillars drop from above in a
  staggered volley.
- Contact: hit stop 40–80 ms, an impact star with speed lines, a dust puff for slams, the target
  knocked along the attack; scratches land as 2–3 quick streaks 60–80 ms apart.
- Dissipation: speed lines gone in a frame or two, the star fades fast, dust rises and thins, gold
  sparks fall; the beam retracts from the root.

## What sells it
- Impact stars from the TCG Live spike/flash textures in cream-gold with a small white core;
  radial speed lines one or two frames.
- Claw streaks as motion-blurred parallel crescents with a brighter edge.
- A dust puff (matter) at a slam; gold sparks with gravity.
- Energy as gold spheres with a Fresnel shell and a stood gold ring; pillars as upright lit
  columns from the sky with a soft cap.
- Crush Grip's hand as a leaned translucent sprite with a lit rim, closing over two frames.

## Card motion & sound
`lunge` / `dash` with a strong drive for tackles (the body carries normal more than any other
type); `brace` for beams. The target `knock` along the attack, `stagger` for multi-swipes. Sound:
a clean thud or crack transient, a short whoosh, a brief dust tail; beams a hum and a boom.

## Not its neighbour
- vs **Fighting**: fighting is orange with red-brown shards and limb stamps; normal is white-gold
  and cleaner.
- vs **Fairy**: fairy glitters pink; normal does not sparkle.
- vs **Steel**: steel is cool silver-blue; normal is warm gold.
- vs **Flying (speed lines)**: flying's lines are wind that continues; normal's are impact lines
  that vanish.

## Reference cues (SV)
- Tackle / Body Slam: a lunge, an impact star with speed lines, a dust puff.
- Scratch / Fury Swipes: quick parallel white claw streaks.
- Hyper Beam: a white-gold beam with a gold halo into a big burst.
- Judgment: a gold orb charges under a gold ring, then gold beams rain on the target into an explosion.
- Crush Grip: a giant white hand closes on the target; gold sparks.
- Multi-Attack: an orange-gold glow and an orange slash arc.

## Failure signatures seen here → fix
- Crush Grip's end flare as a white star → gold sparks and flare, no white core.
- Judgment orbiting the orb with spinning ring arcs ("the spinning circle again") → no rings;
  gold pillars from above.
- Multi-Attack's white blades → gold-orange tint.
- Generic white impact blob on the attacker → a warm rim; the star is at the contact point only.
