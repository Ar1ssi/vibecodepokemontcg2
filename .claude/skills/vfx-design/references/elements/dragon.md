# Dragon — how it reads

**Identity:** fire's structure in a cool, clean palette — violet-magenta energy with cyan/teal
edges and pink cores, in helix beams, pulse orbs, roaring shockwaves and dragon-head silhouettes;
no soot, no embers.

## Physical truth
Dragon energy is a fiction with a firm convention: it behaves like fire (tongues, bursts, a hot
core) but it is *energy*, so it is cleaner (no smoke), cooler in hue (violet → magenta → pink with
a cyan or green edge), and often structured (helixes, concentric pulses, a head shape at a beam's
tip). Signature dragons bring their own hue: crimson (Eternatus), blue-white (Dialga), green-lime
(Zygarde, Rayquaza), gold-pink (Palkia).

## Shape language
- Beams: a pulse beam with a smooth pink-violet body and a brighter core; a helix of two ribbons
  winding around the axis; a stream with a dragon-head silhouette at its tip (Dragon Rage/Breath).
- Orbs and meteors: a violet orb thrown up, magenta meteors raining in curved arcs into a cloud.
- Auras: violet rage flames (fire tongues in violet) around the attacker; a roar shockwave as
  expanding rings of distortion with a pink edge.
- Slashes: dragon-claw triple streaks in violet with a cyan edge.
- Energy orbs filling a scene (Dragon Energy): many magenta orbs, then pink rays.

## Value & light (additive, cool)
Core pink-white `#FFD2F5` (small) → hot magenta `#FF3CF0` / `#E14CFF` → body violet `#BE78FF` /
`#7A2BD6` → deep `#5A1FA6` / `#2E4FE0`. Accent: cyan `#5FD8FF` / `#7FF7FF` on edges and sparks, or
yellow `#FFC46B` sparks (Dragon Breath, Outrage impacts). No soot stop — the deep violet edge is
what makes it read over a bright card; a white slab beam is the recurring failure.

## Palette (sampled)
- deep `#5A1FA6` · body `#BE78FF` · hot `#FF3CF0` · core `#FFD2F5` · accent `#5FD8FF` (Dragon Energy, Dynamax Cannon)
- Eternabeam crimson `#C81EFF #FF2E7A #6A1FD0`; Roar of Time blue `#52E6FF #2E4FE0 #7A2BD6`;
  Spacial Rend pink-gold `#FF7FD9 #FFC46B #9A3CF0`; Core Enforcer lime `#B8FF8A #F2E94A #5FE6F5`.
- Outrage impacts: yellow star bursts on a violet aura.
- Drift to avoid: toward fire's orange (an orange rim from the fire kit), or toward white.

## Motion signature
- Charge (300–600 ms): a violet aura with tongues around the attacker, a pink core at the mouth,
  cyan motes; rage auras pulse.
- Travel: a pulse beam fires in a frame with a white flash at the muzzle (small) and a visible
  pulse running along it; a helix winds at 1–2 turns per lane; meteors arc down over 300–500 ms.
- Contact: a magenta burst with cyan sparks, a pink shockwave ring (stood, with volume), a
  dragon-head silhouette biting at the tip for breath moves; the target knocked with a violet veil.
- Dissipation: energy dissolves to motes and a magenta haze; no embers, no smoke; the aura
  flickers out from the tips.

## What sells it
- Fire's kinds (jets, fireballs) in the dragon palette with the soot stop replaced by a deep violet
  edge and the ramp ending in pink-white.
- Helix beams: two ribbons offset by half a turn around the beam axis, each with a core and a cyan
  edge, scrolling toward the head.
- Pulses: brightness travelling along the beam as a moving band.
- A head silhouette (the TCG Live `T_VFX_Dragon_DragonRage_Head` texture) stood at the beam tip.
- Shockwave rings as stood, leaned sprites with a distortion layer; cyan sparks with drag.

## Card motion & sound
`rear-lurch` with a violet rim and a long hold for the roar; `lunge` for claws. The target `knock`
with a violet veil. Sound: a roar/growl build, a deep boom transient, a shimmering tail; breath
moves a sustained whoosh.

## Not its neighbour
- vs **Fire**: fire is warm (red-orange-yellow), dirty (soot, embers), and dies from the tips; dragon
  is cool (violet-magenta-cyan), clean, and dissolves to motes.
- vs **Psychic**: psychic is rings, lenses and slow swells; dragon is tongues, beams, helixes and roars.
- vs **Poison**: poison's purple is glossy liquid and sick gas; dragon's violet is light.
- vs **Fairy**: fairy is pink-white and soft; dragon's pink is a hot core inside violet.

## Reference cues (SV)
- Dragon Pulse: a violet-pink pulse beam with a white flash; wide.
- Dragon Breath: a pink-violet stream with yellow sparks.
- Draco Meteor: a violet orb thrown up, then magenta meteors rain into a big magenta cloud.
- Outrage: a violet rage aura on the user, then yellow-star impacts on the target.
- Dragon Energy: the screen fills with magenta energy orbs, then pink rays.
- Eternabeam: a crimson-red beam into a dark red haze.

## Failure signatures seen here → fix
- Dragon Pulse / Breath / Dynamax Cannon as white beam slabs or faint blobs → a clear magenta
  (pink-violet) beam, slimmer; the palette shifted to magenta.
- Draco Meteor sending a white pillar up and one flash → a magenta pillar up, a meteor volley from
  the sky, a magenta cloud.
- Outrage showing little at contact → yellow impact streak bursts on the defender.
- Core Enforcer glowing purple with a white blob → a lime/green beam with a green rim and a Z
  streak at the defender (signature hue overrides the type).
- Eternabeam's white X slashes at the user → dropped; crimson beam, crimson tint.
- Nihil Light's yellow grid lattice (clip art) → dropped; a pink-green charge.
