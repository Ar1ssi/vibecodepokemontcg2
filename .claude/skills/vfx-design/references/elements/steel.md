# Steel — how it reads

**Identity:** cool silver metal with a hard, long specular highlight and a blue-grey shadow; it
clangs, throws orange sparks on contact, and its *energy* is blue-white light, not black balls.

## Physical truth
Polished metal has almost no colour of its own: it reflects its environment, so it reads as a
bright anisotropic highlight streaking along its surface, a mid grey body and a dark reflection
of the ground. On a dark board with no environment to reflect, a metal sphere reads as a black
hole — which is why steel *energy* must be drawn as light (a blue-white sphere or beam with a
steel-blue tint), and metal is kept for solid props (a blade, a fist, a hammer, shards). Impacts
on metal throw orange-white sparks that fly fast and die fast.

## Shape language
- Props: a blade (long, straight edge with a highlight streak), a fist/nut-fist stamp, a hammer, a
  steel head; silver shards with facets.
- Energy: a steel-blue/white sphere charged at the source; a slim blue-white beam with silver
  shards and motes along it.
- Sparks: short orange-white dashes from the contact point, fast, with gravity, dying in 150–300 ms.
- Slash streaks: silver-white with a blue tint, thin, motion-blurred.
- Shine: a four-point glint sliding along a blade's edge — one, not a field of stars.

## Value & light (metal matter + blue-white light)
Metal: dark reflection `#3A3F55` → body grey `#9AA7B8` → light grey `#D5DEEA` → highlight white
(long, thin, anisotropic: stretched along the surface direction). Energy (additive): deep steel
blue `#2F8CFF` → body `#7FD8FF` → hot `#E9FFFF` → a small white core. Sparks: orange `#FFB347` /
`#FF8A1F` with a white-hot tip. Never a near-white *fill*: the steel palette's paleness is what
bloomed Steel Beam white.

## Palette (sampled)
- Metal: `#3A3F55 #9AA7B8 #D5DEEA #FFFFFF`; sparks `#FFB347 #FFB13B`; (Double Iron Bash, Mighty Cleave)
- Energy: `#2F8CFF #7FD8FF #E9FFFF` (Behemoth Blade, Tachyon Cutter `#3FE0FF #7FE9FF`); Flash
  Cannon white-cyan orb → blue-white beam.
- Signature accents: Behemoth Bash is a **crimson-orange** aura and a **gold-orange** burst
  (`#FF5A2E #FFB13B #FFF27A`); Behemoth Blade a blue aura with blue-violet `#B26BFF` slash streaks;
  Sunsteel Strike gold `#FFD23F #FF8A1F`; Doom Desire pink-white `#FF2E88` from the sky.
- Drift to avoid: everything toward white (white slabs, white stars) — the recurring steel failure.

## Motion signature
- Charge (200–500 ms): a glint slides along the prop; a steel-blue rim; for energy moves a
  blue-white orb grows at the mouth with a slow spin.
- Drive: a `lunge`/`dash` with a silver streak; a beam fires in a frame and holds slim and bright.
- Contact: a hard metallic clang — hit stop 60–80 ms, orange sparks burst from the point and fall,
  a blue-white flash (small), silver shards tumble; two heavy stamps for double hits.
- Dissipation: sparks die fast (they are the shortest-lived particle of any element), shards fall
  and ring, the highlight fades, a faint blue haze lingers briefly.

## What sells it
- Anisotropic highlight: a specular stretched along the prop's axis (`pow(1 - |dot(T, H)|, n)` with
  the tangent), moving as the prop turns; a dark ground reflection on its lower half.
- Faceted silver shards as real debris with hard glints.
- Energy as light spheres with a steel-blue Fresnel shell and a small white core; beams slim with
  motes and shards *along* them.
- Sparks as velocity-stretched orange sprites with gravity and very short life.
- One sliding four-point glint on a blade edge at the charge peak.

## Card motion & sound
`lunge` with a steel-blue rim for blades and heads; `brace` for cannons. The target `knock` hard,
`stagger` for double bashes. Sound: a metallic clang transient with a ring tail, a scrape/shing on
the charge glint, a spark fizz.

## Not its neighbour
- vs **Ice**: ice's glints are blue-cold and pointlike at facet corners; steel's highlight is a
  long warm-white streak along a surface. Ice is translucent; steel is opaque.
- vs **Normal**: normal is white-gold force; steel energy is steel-*blue*.
- vs **a black ball**: a dark sphere on the board is a hole, not metal — steel energy is light.
- vs **Fighting**: fighting's stamps are warm gold-orange; steel's are silver with orange sparks.

## Reference cues (SV)
- Iron Head: a steel head glints and charges in; a blue-white burst.
- Flash Cannon: a white-cyan orb charges, a blue-white beam fires.
- Steel Beam: a steel-blue beam with silver shards.
- Bullet Punch: blurred white fist outlines fly into a white impact (small, quick).
- Double Iron Bash: two heavy nut-fist punches with white streaks.
- Behemoth Blade: a blue aura, a glowing blade, a dash, blue-violet slash streaks into a bright burst.

## Failure signatures seen here → fix
- Flash Cannon's beam motes drawn as polished metal balls read black → light spheres.
- Steel Beam / Flash Cannon blooming white (near-white palette) → a steel-blue beam tint, slimmer.
- Iron Head swelling a big white orb on the user → a steel-blue charge tint, small.
- Smart Strike's stroked targeting reticle round the attacker (clip art) → dropped.
- Behemoth Bash/Blade as giant white slabs and rings → coloured auras, coloured slash streaks, no
  white, no rings.
- Sunsteel Strike's spiked sun disc (clip art) → a gold fireball body.
- Double Iron Bash's one big white star slab → two fist stamps, no star.
