# Psychic — how it reads

**Identity:** violet-magenta energy that bends space: lenses, rings and ribbons that swell slowly,
saturate, and implode or dissolve; a cyan edge and a white core; the scene dims around it.

## Physical truth
There is no physical psychic energy, so the reference's own conventions are the truth: a
*distortion* of what is behind (a lens, a ripple), concentric rings that pulse outward or
contract, smooth ribbon arcs, and a palette that runs violet → magenta → pink with a cyan edge and
a white core. Motion is slow and smooth with sudden releases. It is light, but a *heavy* light: it
dims its surroundings rather than lighting them.

## Shape language
- Lenses: a large soft sphere that swells around the target, bending the card art behind it, then
  saturates to magenta and splits into overlapping ring-bubbles.
- Rings: thin outlines (wavy for psybeam) that wrap the attacker and pulse; at impact rings expand
  from the strike. Sized to the card, not the board (see failures).
- Ribbons and crescents: smooth magenta-violet arcs; Psycho Cut's crescents slice through with a
  tall white blade flash.
- Beams: thick white-pink core with cyan edge streaks and wavy magenta/yellow outlines where it drills.
- Motes: slow drifting specks in violet and cyan; eyes/stars only when the reference has them.

## Value & light (additive light + distortion)
Core white `#FFFFFF` (small, in the beam core or the implosion point) → hot pink `#FF4FD8` /
`#DB96FC` → body magenta-violet `#C23CFF` / `#B048B0` → deep violet `#4E1288` / `#6848A8`. Cyan
`#3CE8FF` as the one accent on edges and arcs. The scene around the effect *dims* (a local
vignette) so the violet reads; psychic over a bright card needs its deep violet rim.

## Palette (sampled)
- deep `#6848A8` · body `#B048B0` / `#C858D8` · hot `#DB96FC` · core `#FFFFFF` · accent cyan `#3CE8FF`
  (Confusion, Psyblade, Psycho Boost)
- Psybeam `#CE0AE7 #7829D8` with yellow `#C8A020` and blue `#3356BA` outlines; Psystrike adds gold
  `#FFD84A` rings; Zen Headbutt is cyan-white `#88D8D8 #29CECE` with violet `#9C7BBD`.
- Drift to avoid: toward pink-white → fairy.

## Motion signature
- Charge: a ring outline wraps the attacker and its core charges toward white (400–800 ms); a
  small ripple ring pulses beside its head; the scene dims.
- Travel: a beam fires in a frame and *drills* (holds, wavy outlines); a crescent streaks in
  120–200 ms; a lens swells over 300–500 ms around the target with no travel at all.
- Contact: a white blade flash for crescents (one frame); for lenses, saturation to magenta then
  a split into ring-bubbles; rings expand from the strike; the target `float`s (lifts) for
  telekinetic moves.
- Dissipation: implosion to a point or dissolve to slow motes; rings fade; the dim lifts last; the
  target drops back.
- Smooth eases everywhere; releases are sudden.

## What sells it
- A lens kind: fake sphere normal bending the scene behind it (techniques § Refraction) with a
  violet tint and a cyan Fresnel edge; swell with ease-in-out.
- Rings as lit tori or stood sprites with a soft falloff, pulsing in count and radius; never a
  flat ellipse on the cloth.
- Ribbons as smooth splines with a core and a cyan edge, motion-blurred along their path.
- A local vignette (scene dim) that fades in with the charge and out with the settle.
- Motes with zero gravity, slow drift, slight shimmer.

## Card motion & sound
`brace` with a violet rim and the dim; the target `float` (lift, hold, drop) for Psychic/Confusion,
`knock` for beams and cuts. Sound: a rising hum/shimmer for the charge, a muffled bloom on contact
(not a crack), a ringing tail.

## Not its neighbour
- vs **Fairy**: fairy is pink-white with soft stars, hearts and glitter, no distortion; psychic is
  violet-magenta, bends the scene, and dims it.
- vs **Ghost**: ghost is violet *smoke* and cold cyan-green light; psychic is clean violet light
  and rings.
- vs **Water**: both refract; water flows in a stream and is blue; psychic swells in a lens and is
  violet.
- vs **Dragon**: dragon's violet-magenta has fire's tongues and helixes; psychic has rings and lenses.

## Reference cues (SV)
- Confusion: a small pink/cyan ripple beside the attacker's head; a huge soft pale-pink sphere
  swells around the target, saturates to magenta, splits into blue/magenta ring-bubbles.
- Psybeam: a wavy magenta ring wraps the attacker, its core charges white; a thick white-pink
  beam with cyan edge streaks drills in inside wavy magenta and yellow outlines.
- Psycho Cut: a glowing magenta-violet crescent slices through with a tall white blade flash; 2–3
  more from alternating angles.
- Psystrike: gold, pink and violet rings around the target; a flare core; orange spikes.
- Zen Headbutt: a thin white halo with violet/cyan crystal spikes on the head; a pink-white comet.

## Failure signatures seen here → fix
- **"Psychic rings large"** (open verdict): rings sized to the board, not the card → radius
  0.4–0.9 card heights around the target, stood upright, a soft falloff; the ring is a lit torus or
  a leaned sprite, never a flat ellipse on the cloth.
- "Psychic looks like clip art" → the lens kind bending the card art, a real dim, rings with volume.
- Pink-white sparkle reading as fairy → violet body, cyan edge, distortion.
