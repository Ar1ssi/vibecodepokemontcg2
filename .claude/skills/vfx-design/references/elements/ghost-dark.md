# Ghost & Dark — how they read

**Identity (ghost):** violet-indigo smoke and voids with a cold cyan-green light inside; it seeps,
swallows light, and sinks.
**Identity (dark):** crimson-magenta cuts, fangs and claws on near-black; hard-edged, fast, with
black shards; it strikes.

Both are *subtractive*: they darken the scene around them and read against it with a lit rim or a
cold core, not a bright fill. A ghost or dark move with a white burst is a different element.

## Physical truth
Smoke (ghost) is matter: soft, slow, expanding, thinning; it occludes and it takes light on its
edge. A void is an absence: a dark core with a rim. Cold light (ghost's cyan-green, violet) glows
weakly from inside the smoke. Dark's shapes are *cuts*: slashes, fangs, claws — fast, hard, with
a crimson or magenta edge and a black body; its debris are black shards that fall.

## Shape language
- Ghost: drifting smoke puffs and tendrils; a dome or sphere void with a violet rim; black shards
  spinning away; flame-like violet spirit fire (Astral Barrage); eyes/faces only when the
  reference shows them. Lick's tongue is a glossy pink 3D volume.
- Shadow Ball: a *solid* black-violet orb with a violet rim and ribbon arcs around it — never a
  pale or white-rimmed ball.
- Dark: white fang outlines closing (Bite), crimson slash streaks (Night Slash, Wicked Blow), a
  dark fist, black-violet voids (Dark Void), crimson-magenta flame (Fiery Wrath), coins (Pay Day is normal).
- Edges: ghost soft and seeping; dark hard and cutting.

## Value & light (subtractive, with a cold core)
Ghost: deep `#1A0A2E` / `#2E0B4A` body (smoke, void) → violet `#7A3CFF` / `#6B2BD9` rim → pale
`#C9A8FF` haze → cold accent cyan `#7FF7FF` / `#5FF2FF` or mint `#3FD0B0`. The darkest value is in
the effect's core; bloom is limited to the cold accent filaments.
Dark: black `#0E0A0C` / `#1A0A10` body → crimson `#E8142F` / `#E0175A` → magenta `#FF2A7A` /
`#FF4FD8` edge → a gold `#F5C542` / `#FFF08A` accent for impact sparks. The scene dims to a cold
violet or a deep red around the strike.

## Palette (sampled)
- Ghost: Astral Barrage `#7A3CFF #C9A8FF #E43BE0 #1A0A2E #7FF7FF`; Shadow Force `#0E0A14 #6B2BD9
  #B56BFF #5FF2FF`; Moongeist Beam `#E8F7FF #5FE6F5 #7A4FFF #FFD86B #0B1733` (cold white-cyan beam
  with gold).
- Dark: Wicked Blow `#FF3D6E #E8142F #1A0A10 #FFF08A #7A3CFF`; Ruination `#0E0A0C #E0142E #FF2A7A
  #9B2AE0`; Dark Void `#5B2BD6 #B43CFF #FF3DA0 #1A1028`; Hyperspace Fury `#E0189A #FF4FD8 #7A2AB8
  #F5C542`.
- Drift to avoid: ghost toward pink-white (fairy) or clean violet (psychic); dark toward orange
  (fire) or a white flash (normal).

## Motion signature
- Ghost charge: smoke seeps up around the attacker; a cold core lights inside it (300–600 ms); the
  scene dims cold violet.
- Ghost travel: a dense orb or a dome shockwave crosses the lane in 200–300 ms with ribbon arcs;
  smoke trails. Contact: a dark burst — the void expands, black shards spin out, a cyan-white
  crackle cluster decays into violet/black motes that *fall*; a violet haze lingers 400 ms.
- Dark charge: a crouch, a crimson rim, a dark gathering at the claws/jaw (150–300 ms).
- Dark strike: one or several hard cuts in 60–120 ms each (slash streaks, fangs snapping shut), a
  crimson-magenta flash (tinted, not white) and black shards; the target knocked hard.
- Dissipation: smoke thins and sinks; shards fall; the dim lifts last; crimson streaks fade fast.

## What sells it
- Smoke sprites (matter, over) with a dark body, lit *edges* in violet, slow expansion and drift;
  a cold core sprite inside at low alpha.
- A void as a sphere with an inverted value structure: darkest centre, violet Fresnel rim.
- Black shards as real debris with gravity and a rim glint.
- Dark cuts as motion-blurred streaks with a black body and a crimson edge; fangs as hard white
  outlines on black.
- A local cold vignette during the effect.

## Card motion & sound
Ghost: `warp` or `brace`; the target `knock` with a cold veil, or `sink` for traps. Dark: `lunge` /
`dash` with a crimson rim; the target `knock` hard, `stagger` for multi-cuts. Sound: ghost is a
breathy whoosh and a hollow bloom with a whisper tail; dark is a snarl, a sharp cut and a thud.

## Not its neighbour
- Ghost vs **Psychic**: ghost is smoke and voids; psychic is clean light, rings and lenses.
- Ghost vs **Poison**: poison's gas is warm-sick yellow-green and its liquid is glossy; ghost's
  smoke is cold violet and dry.
- Dark vs **Fire**: dark's flame (Fiery Wrath) is crimson-magenta on black with no soot ramp.
- Dark vs **Normal / Fighting**: dark's impact flash is crimson-tinted; theirs is white-gold.

## Reference cues (SV)
- Shadow Ball: violet ribbon arcs round the attacker; a solid black-violet orb flies to a dark burst.
- Astonish: a cyan core bursts into a cyan-white sphere ringed with needle streaks; a violet dome
  shockwave with black shards; a crackle cluster decaying into falling motes.
- Shadow Punch: a long elastic violet arm; a cyan fist from a black-spiked starburst; a magenta
  splash; shards; a violet haze.
- Bite: white fang outlines (a jaw) snap shut on the target.
- Lick: the scene dims cold violet; a huge glossy pink tongue swoops through; a cyan-white
  sparkle puff with purple/black motes.

## Failure signatures seen here → fix
- Shadow Ball pale and white-rimmed → solid dark core, violet rim, no white.
- Astral Barrage's attacker floor rings (clip art) and a hard-outlined dome → drop the rings; fill
  the dome dark with a violet rim.
- Bite slashing pink → white fangs closing.
- Astonish drawing an eye glyph → a small violet burst.
