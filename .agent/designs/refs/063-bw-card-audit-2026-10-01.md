# Gen 5 (Black & White era) card-effect audit — 2026-10-01

Read-only audit of every attack, Ability, Trainer, Stadium, Tool and Special Energy printed in the
Black & White era, run per `docs/card-era-audit-and-fix-process.md` §1. Nothing in the engine was
changed. Findings are filed as I233–I244 in `.agent/ISSUES.md` (numbered after design 062's
I224–I232, which sit on the unmerged branch `claude/friendly-davinci-oy3ko5`). The audit ran
against `main` at 532b818; none of 062's fixes are in that tree.

## Scope and corpus
- Sets (TCGdex ids): Black & White bw1, Emerging Powers bw2, Noble Victories bw3, Next Destinies
  bw4, Dark Explorers bw5, Dragons Exalted bw6, Dragon Vault dv1, Boundaries Crossed bw7, Plasma
  Storm bw8, Plasma Freeze bw9, Plasma Blast bw10, Legendary Treasures bw11 (incl. Radiant
  Collection RC), BW Black Star Promos bwp. Not covered: McDonald's 2011/2012 (reprints).
- pkmncards corpus, 1435 printings (`set:blw,epo,nvi,nxd,dex,drx,drv,bcr,pls,plf` then
  `set:plb,ltr,bwp`), split per `type:`:
  - `out/pkmn-bw-cards.json` — 1287 Pokémon;
  - `out/pkmn-bw-trainer-cards.json` — 132 Trainers (93 unique; 30 Supporter, 69 Item, 22 Tool,
    11 Stadium printings);
  - `out/pkmn-bw-special-energy-cards.json` — 8 Special Energy (5 unique); 8 basic Energy skipped.
- TCGdex snapshot: `out/tcgdex-bw-trainers.json` (132 Trainers: all typed — Item 69, Supporter
  30, Tool 22, Stadium 11; unlike WotC, nothing is untyped). Full details for all 1437 cards were
  fetched into the scratch area; `out/tcgdex-bw-pokemon-corpus.json` re-renders the 1289 TCGdex
  Pokémon in the pkmncards text shape so the gates can run on **what the engine actually
  receives** (§5 lesson 1).
- Gates run on both corpora: `audit:attacks`, `audit:abilities`, `audit:oracle`,
  `audit:trainers`, `audit-all-trainers.mjs`, `audit-all-special-energy.mjs`. Zero engine errors
  in every run.
- Probes (scratch, not committed; described so they can be rebuilt):
  - attack probe — runs one corpus attack on the `attack-harness` rich board with a mutation that
    makes its printed condition true (Special Energy on the defender, Team Plasma marker, Tool in
    play, all-Energy deck, damage on the attacker …), over several seeds incl. heads and tails;
  - TCGdex attack probe — same, using the TCGdex `effect` text;
  - passive spot-check — each "unconsumed" Ability run with and without its text through one
    attack + turn change, or an attach / retreat / useAbility command;
  - rules probe (agent) — TCGdex detail → `ensureCardData` → client `buildCardStatsPayload` →
    server `createCard` + `cardStats` → `applyCommand`, for EX, Restored, Team Plasma, ACE SPEC,
    Dragon, Abilities;
  - Trainer probe (agent) — plays every BW Trainer as TCGdex delivers it, rules on.
  Probes were validated on cards that work (Cobalion-EX Righteous Edge with a DCE attached,
  Bouffalant Revenge with a staged KO victim, Serperior Royal Heal, Sigilyph Safeguard).

## Findings

F1 (I233, P1) — TCGdex BW text breaks passive Ability readers and some attack parsers.
TCGdex prints BW type symbols as words ("any Darkness Energy attached", "is Colorless more",
"provides PsychicPsychic Energy"), has three mojibake/typo rows and one missing space. The damage
parser symbolizes words first (`damage-parser.mjs:370` `symbolizeTypeWords`), but the passive
Ability readers do not, and the typography gate (`scripts/lib/text-variants.mjs`) re-parses
parsers only, never the passive readers. Rebuilding the corpus from TCGdex text flips these rows
from consumed/ok to dead:
- Darkrai-EX Dark Cloak (bw5-63, bw5-107, bw11-88, bwp-BW46): reducer retreat with a {D} Energy
  attached still pays the Retreat Cost (pkmncards `{D}` wording: free).
- Jellicent Stickiness (bw7-45): opponent retreats at the printed cost (`{C}` wording: +1).
- Gardevoir Psychic Mirage (bw4-57, bw5-109), Serperior Royal Garden (bw11-RC3): unconsumed.
- Rayquaza-EX Dragon Burst (bw6-85, bw6-123, bwp-BW47): "Discard all basic Fire Energy or all
  basic Lightning Energy … Energy cards you discarded" → 0 damage, nothing discarded.
- Arcanine Blazing Mane (bw4-12): "Knocked Out),the Attacking" (no space) → unconsumed.
- Claydol Rapid Spin (bw6-64) and Thundurus-EX Thunderous Noise (bw9-110) print U+FFFD
  ("Pok��mon"); Rapid Spin loses its opponent switch. Basculin Splatter (bw2-25) prints
  "oppoent's" → no target, 0 damage.
Fix direction: symbolize type words (and repair U+FFFD → "é") where TCGdex text enters
(`rules-state.mjs` enrichment) or at every reader; extend the typography gate to passive probes.

F2 (I234, P1) — Team Plasma is not recognised on the live data path.
The generated marker table has 113 BW Team Plasma ids (D200), but the client sends no Pokémon
`subtypes` to the server (`client/src/setup/netcode/card-stats.js:95-108`) and only
`search-match.mjs:92` asks `hasCardMarker`. Every other reader matches "plasma" in the name or
subtypes, which BW names never contain. Reproduced: a defender with id bw8-101 (Team Plasma
Snorlax) takes Sawk Kick of Righteousness 10 (expected 50); the same defender with a hand-set
`Team Plasma` subtype takes 50. Affected (cause `path:line`):
- attack conditions and counts — `attack-damage-context.mjs:157-161` `ruleBoxKinds` never calls
  `hasCardMarker`: Sawk (bw10-52), Houndoom Blazing Claws (bw10-56), Haxorus Strike of the
  Champion (bw10-69, also no KO step), Snorlax Teampact (bw8-101), Golurk Iron Fist of Justice (bw10-46, "does nothing" if you have Team Plasma in play — still hits);
- attack targets — `effects/attack-steps.mjs:1006-1010` `rootHasTag`: Thundurus-EX Raiden
  Knuckle (bw9-38, bw9-110, bwp-BW81) finds no target; Raticate Transfer Junk (bw9-88) and
  Volcarona Solar Transporter (bw10-13) do nothing;
- Abilities — Deoxys-EX Power Connect +0 (`ability-combat.mjs:387`); Umbreon Dark Shade gives
  its HP to non-Plasma Pokémon too (`ability-combat.mjs:858-869`); Glaceon Freeze Zone has no
  reader (`ability-combat.mjs:933-956`); Drifblim Drifting Balloon reads the name
  (`ability-executors.mjs:237-238`);
- Trainers/Stadiums/Tools — Frozen City damages Team Plasma Pokémon (`stadium-triggers.mjs:37`),
  Reversal Trigger (`tool-conditions.mjs:107-109`), Silver Mirror blocks non-Plasma attackers
  (`tool-combat.mjs:147-178`), Team Plasma Badge has no reader, Colress Machine parses as a plain
  Pokémon search (`trainer-effects.mjs:693`), Team Plasma Grunt drops its "Discard a Team Plasma
  card" cost (`trainer-effects.mjs:1288`).

F3 (I235, P2) — No BW ACE SPEC is recognised. TCGdex gives them rarity "Rare" and pkmncards
subtype "Item", so `isAceSpecCard` (`card-classify.mjs:143-147`) is false for all 13 (bw7-137…140,
bw8-128…130, bw9-107/108, bw10-92…95). `validateDeck` accepts Computer Search + Dowsing Machine
+ Master Ball (`deck-validation.mjs:124`); Spiritomb Sealing Scream (bw11-87) does not stop Computer
Search (`ability-combat.mjs:1385`). Source fix: an `is:ace-spec` marker in the D200 generator.

F4 (I236, P2) — 18 attacks have no effect beyond damage (Appendix A, "no effect" rows), by
mechanism: coin-outcome damage table (Maractus Constant Rattle); "Flip 2 coins. If both … heads"
(Gothitelle Doom Decree — one coin event, never KOs); "for each … discard the top card of your
opponent's deck" (Durant Devour, Swoobat Jet Woofer); discard → deck top (Trubbish, Durant Pull
Out); opponent puts/shuffles the Defending Pokémon into hand/deck (Carnivine Spit Squall,
Staraptor Strong Breeze); "same amount of damage" (Jellicent Vengeful Wish — the damage-taken
tracking exists, `attack-damage-context.mjs:365`, the wording is unread); Chatot Misinformation
(Tools stay); Azelf Psyjamming (no prompt); Relicanth Fossil Hunt; Ursaring Picnic Weather; plus
the F1/F2 rows. All parse to zero steps in `parseAttackSteps` (`attack-steps.mjs:1699`).

F5 (I237, P2) — 28 attacks run partially (Appendix A, "partial" rows):
- 8 condition phrasings `parseConditionClause` (`attack-conditions.mjs:816`) returns null for:
  "this Pokémon has full HP" (Beedrill), "fewer remaining HP than the Defending Pokémon"
  (Scrafty, Empoleon), "the Defending Pokémon has no Retreat Cost" (Galvantula), "is Burned or
  Poisoned" (Conkeldurr), "remaining HP is 10" (Machoke), "remaining HP is 50 or less" (Archeops,
  base-damage replacement), "any {L} Pokémon on your Bench" (Emolga), "basic Energy … same type
  as the Defending Pokémon" (Ninetales);
- counts the damage parser reports as "resolve the printed count": discard-from-hand ×N (Cofagrigus
  Chuck, Weavile Vilify — no discard prompt), Special Energy in opponent's discard (Drifblim Shadow
  Steal), {R} among milled cards (Heatmor), {W}/{L} in discard + shuffle (Kingdra), minus per {C}
  in Retreat Cost (Throh);
- "Flip 2 coins. If both … heads, discard all Energy" (Altaria/Leavanny Cleave, Umbreon Shadow
  Shutdown); Carnivine Ambush Bite (heads: +20 but no discard); Empoleon Cold Crush (own discard,
  no opponent discard); Thundurus-EX Thunderous Noise (no discard);
- mill-then-attach (Gigalith Shear, Excadrill-EX Dig Out, Rayquaza-EX Celestial Roar);
  Ducklett Roost (no can't-retreat marker); Golurk Devolution Punch (no devolve); Plusle Tag Draw
  ("If Minun is on your Bench, draw 4 more" ignored).

  (`ability-combat.mjs:560-577`) has no target-type filter for "your {M} Pokémon".
- Silver Bangle (bw10-88) adds +30 to every attack: `tool-combat.mjs:288` matches "active
  pokémon ex" without the hyphen and the holder clause "(excluding Pokémon-EX)" is unparsed
  (`tool-conditions.mjs:175-190`).
- Umbreon Dark Shade and Silver Mirror over-apply (F2).

F9 — Trainers, Tools, Stadiums (I241 P1, I243 P2, I244 P2). 95 unique (name, TCGdex effect) Trainers
played through `applyCommand({type:'playTrainer'})` as the live server builds them (loadDeck row
+ cardStats `text=effect`, `trainerType`), rules on and off (identical), seeds 1–12, plus combat,
retreat, KO, Checkup, stadium-effect and attack-cost probes. Controls: modern Rocky Helmet text
puts 20 on the attacker, Bravery Charm refused on a Stage 2, Beach Court 4→3. Result: 57 work,
9 partial, 22 broken, 2 approximated/unsupported (Appendix B). Special Energy 18/18 cost cases
right.
- I241 (P1) BW staples that misplay:
  - Pokémon Catcher (bw2-95, bw5-111, bw10-83): 0 coin flips in 12 seeds and the switch happens
    every time. `parseCoinFlipStep` (`trainer-effects.mjs:1192-1196`) knows only "choose 1 of… /
    switch it with", so the text falls to the coin-less `matchesSwitchOpponentIn` (:508/:1804).
  - Rocky Helmet (bw3-94), Rock Guard (bw7-133/153): never fire; `parseToolOnDamageEffect`
    (`tool-combat.mjs:641`) wants "damaged by an attack", BW prints "an opponent's attack".
  - N (bw3-92, bw5-96, bwp-BW100): hand goes to the deck bottom, no shuffle
    (`executor.mjs:1107-1108` forces `isBottom` for a prize-count draw).
  - Aspertia City Gym (bw7-127): −10 HP instead of +20 to Colorless — "less" inside "Colorless"
    hits the negative branch of `parseStadiumHpModifier` (`stadium-effects.mjs:1545`).
  - Virbank City Gym (bw8-126): server Checkup poison is a flat 10 (`reduce.mjs:2705-2707`); only
    the client `getStadiumCheckupPoisonBonus` (`stadium-effects.mjs:1984`) reads it.
  - Battle City (bwp-BW39): the "Discard this card if another Stadium…" boilerplate matches the
    `discard-draw` branch (`stadium-effects.mjs:1149-1157`) → heads discards 2 from hand.
- I243 (P2) parses and conditions:
  - Dark Patch (bw5-93): type word "Darkness" — filter at `trainer-effects.mjs:2055` reads only
    `{d}`; offers every basic Energy and every Benched Pokémon (F1 family).
  - Search filters guessed from the whole text (`parseSearchDeckKind`, `trainer-effects.mjs:977`):
    Colress Machine (bw8-119) attaches a Pokémon as "Energy"; Heavy Ball (bw4-88) drops "Retreat
    Cost of 3 or more".
  - Costs not parsed or not blocked: Team Plasma Grunt (bw8-125, `trainer-effects.mjs:1338-1344`,
    `trainer-play-conditions.mjs:52`), Pokémon Communication (bw1-99, hand Pokémon never returned,
    playable with none), Superior Energy Retrieval (bw9-103, offers the card just discarded),
    Scramble Switch (bw8-129, Energy move dropped).
  - Pokédex (bw1-98): misses `rearrangeTop` (`trainer-effects.mjs:3115`) and becomes "take 1, then
    shuffle". Hooligans Jim & Cas (bw5-95): picks from the revealed hand instead of at random
    (`trainer-steps.mjs:2763-2779`).
  - Tool holder conditions unparsed (`parseHolderPhrase`, `tool-conditions.mjs:141-142`): Eviolite
    (bw3-91, bw8-122) also on a Stage 2, Dark Claw (bw5-92) and Crystal Edge (bw7-138) on any
    holder; Silver Bangle / Silver Mirror (F7/F2).
  - Unimplemented: G Booster, G Scope (bw10-92/93 — TCGdex gives only the attack body;
    `parseGrantedAttacks`, `tool-attacks.mjs:68-75`, knows TS-1/TS-2 only), Victory Piece
    (bw8-130), Crystal Wall (bw7-139), First Ticket (dv1-19, playable mid-game and wasted).
    Cedric Juniper (bw11-110) is approximated. Revive (bw1-102) offers a Restored Pokémon as a
    Basic — needs a cited ruling before anyone changes it.
- I244 (P2, not era-specific): a second Tool can be attached through `playTrainer` with a
  `targetInstanceId` (`trainer.mjs:231`); the `attachCard` path and the prompt path refuse it.
Works (sample): Stadiums Skyarrow Bridge, Plasma Frigate, Pokémon Center, Tropical Beach, Twist
Mountain, Champions Festival; Tools Float Stone, Giant Cape, Rescue Scarf, Life Dew, Exp. Share;
Fossil Items bench Restored Pokémon; hand-discard costs (Ultra Ball, Computer Search, Dowsing
Machine) block when unpayable; Iris, Hypnotoxic Laser, Ghetsis, Shadow Triad, Team Plasma Ball,
Elesa, Level Ball and the coin Items behave.

F10 (I242, P3) — Data quirks: bwp-BW78 Raichu is Stage1 with no `evolveFrom`, so it evolves onto
any Pokémon (`reduce.mjs:4508-4516` skips the name check when `evolvesFrom` is null); bw11-RC23,
bwp-BW51/76/91 have no `retreat` field and retreat for free (not verified against print).

## Not findings
- Pokémon-EX: all 112 are Basic (TCGdex has no `stage`; enrichment infers it), give 2 Prizes, and
  EX-gated shields/bonuses work (Safeguard, Gold Breaker, Plasma Steel's EX gate, Steel Slash,
  Electri-Defuse). The server recognises EX by the "-EX" name (no `subtypes` arrive); every BW EX
  name ends that way. EX vs EX-era "ex" are deliberately the same (D74).
- Restored Pokémon (6) can't be played from hand; Plume/Cover Fossil, Old Amber Aerodactyl, Root
  Fossil Lileep bench them; Carracosta/Archeops/Cradily evolve onto them.
- Dragon: 87 Dragons, Weakness ×2, mixed costs and Blend Energy payment correct.
- Abilities: all 184 typed `Ability`, no BW card prints two; Ditto Transform works.
- Special Energy: Blend ×2, DCE, Plasma, Prism parse the same on TCGdex text.
- pkmncards-only typos (engine never sees them): Amoonguss/Foongus Astonish ("a random card …
  that cards"), Black Kyurem-EX Dragon Fang ("This a coin", bw7-101), Solosis Cell Culture ("into
  your Bench"), Vaporeon Spiral Drain ("Recover"), Reshiram Outrage, Zoroark Snarl. TCGdex text
  parses correctly for all of them.
- Gate rows that work once the condition is staged (harness artifacts): Special-Energy discards
  (Cobalion-EX, Kyurem-EX, Garchomp, Drifblim Derail), Latios/Latias, Reshiram/Zekrom Fusion,
  Nidoking Lovestrike, Simipour/Simisear, draw-until-6 (Espeon, Klinklang, Lilligant), Articuno-EX
  Frost Prison, Dialga-EX Fast Forward, Liepard Assist.
